## Key Insights

**Foiling as deliberate practice:**
Josh uses an e-foil with a folding propeller to practice specific surfing maneuvers (like handling boils) over 200 times in two sessions — equivalent to 1-2 months of regular surfing progress.

> Afterwards, I feel like I have to lie in a dark room with my eyes closed for 10, 15 minutes just to process. The brain feels like it's plugged into the matrix.

**The most important turns:**
Billy Kidd asked Josh: "What are the three most important turns of a ski run?"

The answer: **The last three before getting on the lift.** Most skiers get sloppy at the end, but your last actions are what your brain internalizes overnight. Always finish strong.

**HRV and fear response:**
Josh uses resonant frequency breathing to override his body's impulse to bail on steep waves. Over time, the baseline rises and you can handle increasingly intense conditions.

**Trigger work:**
Getting into a peak performance state and attaching a trigger (song, scent, breath) so you can enter that state instantly instead of needing a 30-minute routine.
