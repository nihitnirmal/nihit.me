# Good to Great

![rw-book-cover](https://media.shortform.com/covers/png/good-to-great-cover.png)

**Author:** Jim Collins

## Highlights
- People can be divided into foxes and hedgehogs: Foxes know many things and engage with a complex world on its own terms; hedgehogs know one big thing and subsume the world’s complexity to a single, simple, unifying idea. ([View Highlight](https://www.shortform.com/app/highlights/4a636b35-00e8-4d6d-b91a-d7867339a884))
- Collins et al. determined that “hedgehog concepts” emerge from a Venn diagram consisting of three key questions: What can I do better than anyone else in the world? What is my financial engine? And: What am I profoundly passionate about? ([View Highlight](https://www.shortform.com/app/highlights/33021075-3981-4adf-9fa4-656c88fc07e7))
